# Privacy and trust contract

- The website never receives a user's complete ChatGPT or other model-provider memory.
- It receives only structured context the active agent chooses to pass into a tool call.
- Agent-supplied context is visibly attributed.
- Current-search context may affect the present ranking without becoming durable.
- Saving a preference requires a separate human approval.
- Anonymous use is complete and does not require an identity provider.
- Sign in with Google or ChatGPT, if later implemented, is an identity boundary rather than a memory-transfer mechanism.
- Precise private addresses are not inferred from vague context. Approximate areas or public destinations are preferred when sufficient.
- External listing content is untrusted, bounded, and source-attributed.
- No applications, landlord messages, bookings, payments, or lease signatures are available to an agent.
